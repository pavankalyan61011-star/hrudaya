## Why you were in hospital
You had a heart attack, called ST-elevation myocardial infarction (STEMI). This is an emergency in which a blood vessel supplying the heart gets blocked. You were in hospital from 2026-09-24 to 2026-09-27. Your case code is PCI-2026-011.

## What was done
Your doctors took an X-ray video of the heart arteries (an angiogram). The thin tube was passed through the artery at your right wrist (radial route). One drug-eluting stent was placed in the proximal LCX (left circumflex artery, a main branch on the left side of the heart). A stent is a small mesh tube that holds the artery open and slowly releases medicine. Balloons were also used to open the artery and fit the stent.

## What we found
- LMCA (left main coronary artery): 0% narrowing.
- LAD (left anterior descending artery): 0% narrowing.
- RCA (right coronary artery): 10% narrowing.
- Proximal LCX: 100% narrowing. This artery was completely blocked, with no blood flow through it.

After the stent was placed, the narrowing in the proximal LCX was 5%, and blood flow was normal again.

## Your heart pump
Your heart's pumping strength (LVEF) was 55%.
