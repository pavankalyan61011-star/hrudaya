## Why you were in hospital

You had a heart attack, known as an ST-elevation myocardial infarction (STEMI). This is an emergency in which a blood vessel supplying the heart gets blocked. You were in hospital from 2026-09-26 to 2026-09-30. Your case code is PCI-2026-012.

## What was done

Your doctors passed a thin tube through an artery in your right groin to take pictures of the heart's blood vessels (an angiogram). They found the blocked vessel and opened it. A drug-eluting stent, a small mesh tube that slowly releases medicine, was placed in the Mid LAD (the left anterior descending artery, a main vessel on the front of the heart). One stent was used.

## What we found

- **LMCA** (left main coronary artery): 0% narrowing.
- **Mid LAD**: 100% narrowing, fully blocked. After the stent, about 5% narrowing remained.
- **Proximal LCX** (left circumflex artery): 75% narrowing.
- **Mid RCA** (right coronary artery): 30% narrowing.

## Your heart pump

Your heart's pumping strength (LVEF) was measured at 45%.
