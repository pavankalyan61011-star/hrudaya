## Why you were in hospital

You had a stress test that was abnormal. This planned procedure was done to look at the arteries of your heart. You also have high blood pressure.

## What was done

You had a coronary angiogram (an X-ray test that shows the arteries of the heart). The doctor reached your heart through an artery in your right wrist (radial access). No stent or balloon treatment was needed.

## What we found

Your heart arteries showed no narrowing (0%) and blood flow was normal in all of them:

- LMCA (left main coronary artery): 0% narrowing
- LAD (left anterior descending artery): 0% narrowing
- LCX (left circumflex artery): 0% narrowing

## Looking after yourself

- If you still have symptoms, your doctors will look at other possible causes, including problems in the very small heart vessels, spasm of the arteries, or causes outside the heart.
- Take care of the wrist where the procedure was done.

## When to come back urgently

**Go to the nearest hospital emergency department or call 108 immediately if you have:**

- Chest pain or pressure lasting more than a few minutes, or coming back
- Severe breathlessness
- Fainting or collapse
- Bleeding from the wrist puncture site that does not stop after 10 minutes of firm pressure
- A rapidly growing swelling at the puncture site
- The hand on that side becoming cold, pale, numb or very painful
- Black or bloody stools, or vomiting blood

**Contact the cardiology team within a day for:**

- Increasing pain, redness, warmth or discharge at the puncture site
- Fever
