## Why you were in hospital

You had a heart attack called NSTEMI (non-ST-elevation myocardial infarction). It needed urgent care. You came in on 2026-08-31 and went home on 2026-09-02. Your case code is PCI-2026-001.

## What was done

The doctor reached your heart through the artery in your right wrist (radial access). Dye was used to take pictures of the arteries of your heart (angiogram). One drug-eluting stent was placed in the proximal LAD (left anterior descending artery, a main artery on the front of the heart). A stent is a small mesh tube that holds the artery open. Balloons were also used to open and prepare the artery.

## What we found

- **LMCA (left main coronary artery):** 0% narrowing.
- **Proximal LAD:** 90% narrowing. This was the blocked artery. After the stent, no narrowing was left and blood flow was normal.
- **Mid LCX (left circumflex artery):** 40% narrowing.
- **Proximal RCA (right coronary artery):** 30% narrowing.

- Stop smoking.
- Join a cardiac rehabilitation programme.
- Eat a heart-healthy diet.
- Return to physical activity slowly, step by step.
